# TINELLA — SWOT

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|
| Access to a real stone fabricator and potentially local offcuts | No verified product-specific CNC costs or demand |
| Clear design language in mass/void/proportion | Stone can chip, stain, crack and be difficult to ship |
| Small-to-large coherent product family | Furniture designs not structurally engineered |
| Genuine provenance and authenticity | Limited brand distribution and product photography initially |

| Opportunities | Threats |
|---|---|
| Architects/specifiers and premium interiors | Established brands like Salvatori, Marsotto and Editions Milano |
| Offcut-derived low-waste products | Ceramic and porcelain imitators at lower price |
| Limited editions and made-to-order furniture | Freight damage, returns and long lead times |
| Lighting and custom B2B commissions | Product-safety liability, copycats, demand cyclicality |

## Practical mitigations
Pilot 4 small objects; test packaging; maintain provenance/stone documentation; perform pre-production CNC trials; commission furniture engineering, test joinery and use protected packaging; do not launch lighting without certification assessment.
