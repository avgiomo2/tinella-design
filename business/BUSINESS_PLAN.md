# TINELLA — Business Plan, Working Hypotheses

## Positioning
Premium natural-stone objects made with Marmi Cave Tinella using original, architecturally composed forms, clear provenance, and an efficient CNC workflow. Launch objects first; expand to larger furniture after evidence of demand and engineering validity.

## Collections
- 01 Small objects: VUOTO, SOGLIA, TORRE, PIEGA.
- 02 Furniture: ARCO, BLOCCO, MASSA, SEDUTA.
- 03 BAMBOO: marble-pipe lounge chair and triple sofa, removable MAREMMA linen cushions.
- 04 Lighting: research only, subject to electronics and safety compliance.

## Customer/channel
DTC design website, architects/interior designers, high-end stores, galleries, hospitality and trade commissions. Do not grant exclusive distribution before market evidence.

## Economics: planning only (not partner quotations)
Illustrative initial working capital **€60,000**, which may cover CAD, prototypes, machining, photography, insurance/compliance, packaging, site, retailer samples and contingency; budget must be validated line by line.

Illustrative Year 1 scenarios (EUR, before tax, initial investment and full founder compensation):
| Scenario | Revenue | Operating result |
|---|---:|---:|
| Conservative | 16,200 | -26,240 |
| Base | 62,750 | -11,940 |
| Upside | 175,500 | +35,700 |

These are scenario outputs from the initial planning discussion, **not forecasts**. Base case not yet profitable. An earlier illustrative break-even estimate was 504 small-object units per year conditional on 12 furniture sales and assumed unit margins; it must be recalculated once costs and channel discounts are known.

## Unit economics template
For every SKU track: rough stone blank, quarry preparation, CNC machine minutes, tool consumption, finishing, scrap %, QC, assembly inserts, labor, packaging, inbound/outbound freight, damage reserves, VAT, trade discounts, retailer payment terms and returns.
Contribution per unit = net revenue (ex VAT) - all variable costs.
Break-even unit volume = fixed operating costs / weighted contribution margin per unit, after modelling furniture and mixed-channel volumes.

## Critical constraints
Demand may not support premium prices; stone stock varies; furniture mass and brittle materials raise risk. No claim of sustainability metrics until waste reduction and energy use are measured. Record manufacturing origin correctly.

## Go/no-go
Before pilot launch require: 4 matching CAD/flat/render sets; real CNC sample cost; safe prototypes; stone-specific QC; verified trade intent or deposits; tested shipping cartons; gross margin target by channel; clear wholesale terms.
