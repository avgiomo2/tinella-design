# TINELLA — 90-Day Validation Roadmap

## Days 1–14
- Confirm the actual stone selection, stock / quarry blocks, CNC envelope, 3-axis versus 5-axis capability, drilling and finish options.
- Define single source of truth for each SKU: parametric CAD and projected views.
- Verify Monolith, Salvatori, Marsotto and other material/retail comparables with date-stamped links.
- Collect real rough material and machine-hour costs.

## Days 15–30
- Build four geometrically consistent small-object prototypes: VUOTO, SOGLIA, TORRE, PIEGA.
- Measure milling time, scrap, finish time, mass, edge strength, waterproofing and packaging damage.
- Reconcile prototype against CAD revisions.
- Prototype an isolated BAMBOO elbow + straight sleeve + joint before committing to a chair.

## Days 31–60
- Produce samples, technical data sheets and final professional photos based on CAD and physical pieces.
- Interview 20 potential design retailers and 10 architects/specifiers.
- Test prices, sampling/wholesale terms and deposit/preorder intent.
- Prepare minimum viable website and lead list.

## Days 61–90
- Decide which 2–4 SKUs go to a pilot.
- Audit actual gross margins and channel economics.
- Validate packaging, QC, shipping and warranty.
- Make separate engineered furniture feasibility plan.
- Do not claim launch readiness without manufacturing and safety approvals.

## Track
Lead → sample request → proposal → order → deposit → fulfillment → repeat order. Separate engagement indicators from realized sales.
