# TINELLA — Objects in Stone

A collaborative design, engineering, and business research workspace for natural-stone objects with Marmi Cave Tinella (Locorotondo, Puglia, Italy).

## Project scope

- Small CNC stone objects: **VUOTO, SOGLIA, TORRE, PIEGA**.
- Furniture: **SEDUTA, ARCO, MASSA, BLOCCO**.
- **BAMBOO** lounge chair and three-seat sofa: cylindrical stone members, cylindrical stone feet, no ropes or casters, and removable cushions specified in **C&C Milano MAREMMA** linen (exact article/color still to be approved).
- Architectural and mathematical design research: φ, Fibonacci, root rectangles, primes, historical proportion systems, and critical assessment of claims.
- Competitor map, SWOT, manufacturing strategy, business model, 90-day validation plan.

## Critical geometry rule

Earlier generated renderings and flat drawings **did not correspond reliably** (notably SOGLIA, SEDUTA, ARCO and MASSA). They are historical concept studies **not approved for fabrication**. Future work must build parametric CAD geometry first; derive top/front/side/sections and Blender renders from the exact same evaluated model. No fabricated dimension strings inside AI imagery.

**ARCO:** explore two arches meeting at 45° in top section; solve geometry coherently in 3D rather than drawing unrelated views.

## Material integrity

Natural stone means real quarried stone, not gres/porcelain, ceramic, cast stone, resin or stone-effect composites. Hybrid products (e.g. real marble lamp bases) must be marked as hybrid. Confirm with quarry/test documentation before CNC manufacture.

## Reference direction

- [Monolith M_010 Dining Table](https://monolith.nyc/products/010-dining-table) — strong architectural reference. Travertine/lava stone variants are real stone; other variants (wood) should not be counted in natural-stone-only rankings.
- [Monolith product catalogue](https://monolith.nyc/products) — lamps, tables, chairs, trays.
- [Marmi Cave Tinella](https://marmicavetinella.com/).

All design proposals are exploratory until tested for stability, stone strength, connectors, production tolerances, and retail demand. Sales data for private design brands are generally unavailable: rankings must distinguish evidence of unit sales from qualitative visibility signals.
