# Shared working contract: ChatGPT / Claude / CAD / Market

## Source of truth
GitHub is the shared written project ledger. CAD model parameters, not an AI image or a flat sketch, are the dimensional truth.

## Agents
- **Research:** validates real-stone products and source links; clearly identifies retail visibility versus sales facts.
- **Design:** translates historical proportion ideas into original sculptural objects without imitating other firms.
- **CAD:** maintains one solid that drives every elevation and rendering.
- **Engineering:** reviews load path, stone brittleness, joints, packaging, adhesives/isolators and safe operation.
- **Commercial:** manages competitor evidence, verified quotations, unit economics, SWOT and launch gates.

## Deliverable checklist
- source link and verification date for external claims
- revision, datum, units, material and unresolved assumptions
- geometry reconciliation across all views
- physical manufacturability, assembly access and transportability
- clear distinctions between render, proposed CAD and shop-approved CAD
- original intellectual-property work
- reviews recorded before marking a design ready to prototype

## Branch convention
`design/<object>-<short-topic>`, `research/<topic>`, `business/<topic>`. Prefer reviewed PRs for consequential changes.
