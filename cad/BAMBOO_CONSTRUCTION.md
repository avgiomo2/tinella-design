# BAMBOO chair and modular 3-seater — engineering concept

## Visual intent
Circular pale stone frame designed to evoke segmented bamboo. Low, thick seat and back cushions in actual C&C Milano MAREMMA linen, with the appropriate SKU selected by sample. **No showroom rope, no wheels.** Cilindrical stone feet matching the exposed frame.

## Preliminary dimensions (not approved)
One module approximately 780 W x 820 D x 720 H mm; historic concept figures included Ø70 mm visible tube with Ø46 mm central bore and metal frame OD38 mm. These were exploratory assumptions and *must not be treated as approved structure or drilled dimensions*.

## Structural concept
A concealed continuous metal load-bearing chassis carries seat/back load, while separate stone sleeves and corner nodes act as cladding. Isolators separate stone and metal. Stone components are replaceable where possible. Connections, tolerances, adhesives, anti-rotation, stiffness and failure safety remain unresolved.

## Manufacturing research
1. Prototype one tube and one elbow only.
2. Test CNC milling, core drilling, chamfer radii and stone loss.
3. Confirm hidden steel/aluminium frame connection and saddle supports.
4. Evaluate stone wall durability with engineer.
5. Provide independent feet with slip-resistant interfaces and safe restraint against tipping.
6. Test packaging and lift procedures.
7. Only after those tests, model the full single seat and linked triple configuration.

### Technical drawing separation
Keep upholstery as reserved volumes and load-support geometries in engineering flat drawings. Show full upholstered cushions only in separate Blender visualizations. Keep dimensions and shape derived from the same source model.

No prior concept STEP package is approved as a manufacturing file.
