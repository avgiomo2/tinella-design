# Stone Design Market — Competitor Study

## Evidence rule
Private companies generally do not publish product-level sales. Do not present search ranking as actual turnover. Separate **qualitative popularity / distribution / design recognition** from **verified sales**. A product must be individually verified as quarried stone (or marked as hybrid).

## Monolith M_010 Dining Table — verified product information
Original listing: https://monolith.nyc/products/010-dining-table

The product description presents a monumental table supported by **four pillars**. The product page lists **L310 × W100 × H76 cm**, **775 kg**, **travertine and lava rock** material options, **Mexico** as origin, and an **8–10 week** lead time. Designer shown as Monolith Studio. Caveat: text describing a single block should not be assumed to reveal the actual workshop assembly or its structural joints.

Use as a strong reference for **MASSA**'s architectural scale and subtractive stone vocabulary; *do not copy the table's specific construction*. The 775 kg weight also makes transport/access an essential differentiator for TINELLA.

**Sales status:** no independent item-level unit sales or revenue verified; don't rank Monolith above mass-retail lighting on sales without data.

## False positives / material exclusions
101 Copenhagen Sphere ceramic vessels and Atelier Vierkant clay pieces are not natural-stone competitors. Mark gres, porcelain, ceramic, cast stone and synthetic composites as adjacent rather than placing them in a stone-only product database.

## Research next steps
For each company: specific SKU URL, exact composition, photos where licensed, country, price/currency/date, number of independent stockists, designer, dimensions, weight, shipping, presence of public revenue data and independent review sources. Qualitative ranking requires an explicit scoring model and multiple sources.
