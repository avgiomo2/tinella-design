# Natural Stone Verification

Allowed as natural stone when confirmed: marble, limestone, travertine, onyx, alabaster, granite, basalt, lava rock and comparable geological materials.

Disallowed in natural-stone-only counts: gres, porcelain, ceramics, stoneware, concrete, cast stone, stone-effect paint, resin, terrazzo composites and unverified descriptions.

Hybrids: record the real-stone component and total product construction, e.g. marble-base lamp with metal hood.

Verification fields: `brand, product, product_url, material_claim, type(natural_stone/hybrid/excluded/unverified), supporting_quote, source_date, confidence, price, currency, price_date, dimension_mm, weight_kg, distribution_signal, sales_signal`.

TINELLA stone specification remains provisional until the company's actual sample and mechanical sheet are available.
