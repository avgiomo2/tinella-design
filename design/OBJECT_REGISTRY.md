### Geometry rules
Golden ratio, Fibonacci, root rectangles and primes may govern a chosen relationship but may not override ergonomic requirements, CNC tooling, transport or structural engineering.

### BAMBOO special constraints
User reference photos show three low tubular framed lounge seats with seat and back cushions. Remove the showroom rope. **No wheels.** Cylindrical feet of the same visible stone as the members. A structural metal internal frame is one engineering hypothesis, not a validated design. Perform an actual load-path and joint study; ensure fabric support and washable removable cushion access.
